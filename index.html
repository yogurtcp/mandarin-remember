Help

  1. Start with due reviews.
  2. Say the answer before revealing it.
  3. Rate that attempt. A hint counts as help.
  4. Use Learn 5 more whenever you want more new cards.
  5. During recall, roughly every other task uses a sentence containing the word being reviewed, when a suitable sentence is available. Its scored words must already be learned; connecting words are explained with the answer. Sentence grades update the word schedules. Learn 5 more stays word-only, including its short recall steps. Extra review keeps due dates unchanged.
  6. Regular Mandarin includes four-choice pinyin tone questions. Tap Practise tones for a focused batch. Answers are checked automatically, including neutral tones, and have a separate schedule from speaking and meaning recall. These questions test the card’s written pinyin, not a recording of your voice.
  7. For reading, open Learn Chinese characters & compounds. Recognize base characters, then unlock words made from characters you can read. Pinyin and meaning are revealed after recall; no handwriting or typing is required.
  8. Use a phrase today. Come back tomorrow.
Ratings & reviews

Missed: check the answer and retry later. Effortful: correct but hard. Got it: correct. Easy: immediate.

Speaking and understanding have separate schedules. Mistakes return sooner. Extra reviews do not delay scheduled reviews. The daily plan pauses new cards when reviews pile up; “Learn 5 more” lets you choose an extra batch.

Why spaced recall?

Retrieval and practice across days support retention. The app’s exact intervals are practical defaults.
