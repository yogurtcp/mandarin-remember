Help

  1. Start with due reviews.
  2. Say the answer before revealing it.
  3. Rate that attempt. A hint counts as help.
  4. Use Learn 5 more whenever you want more new cards.
  5. Use a phrase today. Come back tomorrow.
Ratings & reviews

Missed: check the answer and retry later. Effortful: correct but hard. Got it: correct. Easy: immediate.

Speaking and understanding have separate schedules. Mistakes return sooner. Extra reviews do not delay scheduled reviews. The daily plan pauses new cards when reviews pile up; “Learn 5 more” lets you choose an extra batch.

Why spaced recall?

Retrieval and practice across days support retention. The app’s exact intervals are practical defaults.
